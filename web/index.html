Solver mode — this is not the BAC model. The dataset network is switched out of the driving seat entirely; what plays you here is pure alpha-beta search with a long clock and a transposition table that carries over between moves. Connect 4 is a solved game (first player wins by move 41), and from the midgame on this search reaches that truth exactly — the proven badge lights up only when it really did. Just for fun. Try beating it.

The numbered outlines are a prediction, not pieces. They are the line the solver expects if both sides play its best moves: 1 is the next move, 2 the reply, and so on. Yellow outlines are yours, red are the bot's. Nothing is played until you click.

Difficulty 0–5 is the n-th best true move, never random noise — even at 0 the bot always takes a win on the board and never walks into a mate in two. 6 is solver mode.
